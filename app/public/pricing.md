# Pricing

Choreo Planer is free. There is no paid plan, no expiring trial, and no feature
behind a paywall.

## Free - 0 EUR

Everything the tool offers is available at no cost:

- Choreography ("choreo") planning with countsheets
- Counts per position and per part
- Teams, clubs, members and positions
- Seasons and season-team assignments
- Lineups
- PDF export and video export
- Access to the public REST API and the MCP server
- Export of your own content

## Why it is free

Choreo Planer is a free online tool for choreography sport. It is built and
maintained as a side project and paid for by its maintainer, so there is nothing
to sell and no tier to upgrade to.
